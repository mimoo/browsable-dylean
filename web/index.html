Tap an underlined symbol to jump to its definition. Use ← Back or your browser’s back gesture to return to your exact reading position.

Open Inspect to explore callers, callees, and all reference locations. Expand a branch with ▸ to walk the hierarchy.

In Lean, callers and callees are declaration dependencies: they include uses in types, proofs, and implementations, not only runtime function calls. These are resolved references recorded by Lean; implicit/generated dependencies without a recorded source reference may be absent.
